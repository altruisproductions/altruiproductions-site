Creator

A solo passion project

BECOME LIFE is an independent project developed by Giovanni Altrui since May 2023. No budget, one creator balancing work, life and fatherhood while building a living story as a game.

"I did not create this game to prove something. I created it to tell a story that could become a place, something to cross and something to live."

This work is fiction. All characters, events and places are imaginary.
